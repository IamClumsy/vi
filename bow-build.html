What this shows:

  • Weapon Mastery (27.5%) is your largest single source of Damage, ahead of Apex Predator (17%).
  • Attack is about half stat points (365) and half gear (482), then five multipliers worth about +30% together. All Stats is the biggest of them at 9.4%.
  • Each STR or INT from a rune adds 1.5 Attack before multipliers, so a Mighty or Sage T4 upgrade is worth a little Attack on all 12 runes.
  • Crit chance is two-thirds gear (44.9 of 67.8), so a piece that drops crit costs more than it looks.
  • Crit damage starts from a 200% base; gear adds 128.6 and your trees 43.4.
  • Execute comes entirely from the bow and its Executioner rune.
  • The Quest Tree adds small amounts almost everywhere: ability damage 8%, Attack 4.5%, attack speed 3.48%, crit damage 2%, CDR 2%.
  • Mana regen is almost all Party Wisdom (75 of 80.8); solo it falls to about 5.8 per 10 s.

Every attack, crit and ability damage node you can see is already maxed. The open nodes are defense, mana and utility, so none adds DPS. With 17 QP, the best known buy is Rift Delver at 20 QP (3 more): an extra Void Rift run every day is more loot and gear chances, which beats any defense node.

Weapon Mastery is the only uncapped damage node. Each new level adds about +0.15% attack speed (about +0.05% DPS) and shrinks as it climbs, so it is the long-term sink, not a priority. Spend order: Rift Delver at 20 QP, then every QP into Weapon Mastery. The totals on your stat sheet check out: Attack 4.5% = Sharp Strikes 2 + Power Surge 1.5 + Warlord 1; attack speed 4.07% = Weapon Mastery; ability damage 8% = Focused Strike 3 + Arcane Mastery 5.

Auras: both apply only to your base stats, so they're smaller than their tooltips say. Attack Aura Lv16 (+4.84%) measured +37 Attack (1,153 → 1,190), worth about +3.2% damage. Haste Aura Lv9 (+2.65%) measured 1.09 → 1.08 s, worth about +0.5%. Run Attack solo. Confirmed in-game: only the strongest aura of each type applies. In a party, run whichever aura type no one else covers: if someone already runs a stronger Attack aura (for example +5.57% against your +4.84%), yours adds nothing, so switch to Haste. A party Wisdom aura is worth about +6–9%, because it keeps your abilities supplied with mana.

Situations checked

  • Single target in a party: this build is best. It skips defensive nodes on purpose.
  • Packs of 3–5 mobs (AoE): the planner's optimizer picks the same build. Arrow Shower's share of your damage rises with pack size.
  • Teammate brings vulnerable: the same build is still best. Your own Hunter's Mark matters less then.

Testing a change

  • Press RESET in Combat Stats.
  • Fight normal packs for 3 minutes or more, with no Champion.
  • Screenshot Session, and compare against a teammate's DPS in the same run, since whole-party swings mean the fight changed.
  • Swap the one change, reset, and repeat.

General tree priority

Best General tree nodes, first to last, with value per point measured from your Combat Stats.

  • Inner Focus: +1% all damage per rank, about +0.65% DPS per point. Maxed at 4/4.
  • Malice: +1.5% crit damage per rank, about +0.38% DPS per point. Malice Path gives the same +1.5% per rank. Every Malice you have is 2/2; the next one is in column 3, behind an Aim.
  • Edge: +0.25% crit chance per rank, about +0.15% per point. Every Edge is 1/2, so rank 2 is the filler.
  • Power: about +0.17% per point.
  • Haste: +0.25% attack speed per rank, about +0.1% per point or less. Keep Haste at 1/2 only where it sits on a path to better nodes.
  • Aim: hit is now 94.25%, so an Aim rank is worth about the same as Edge until you take Eagle Sight. After that, skip Aim.

The Thrift, Well, Spring, Siphon and Grace nodes on the right are mana and sustain nodes (Path of Flow is mana regeneration, Path of Stone is max HP and defense), and none of them beat the damage nodes above, so they're not part of this list. The Focus nodes at the top of each column (Power Focus +0.5% damage, Haste Focus +0.5% attack speed) are already taken and are the best value per point. Resets have been free so far, so moving points is safe to try: check Combat Stats after each batch.

T4 gives about 1.4 times the T3 value (Brutal: +10% at T3, +14% at T4), so each T3-to-T4 upgrade adds roughly 0.4 times the rune's DPS above. Spend gold in this order:

  • Mighty and Sage T4 on armor, about +0.5% each.
  • Done: Precise T4 on the bow (+2% crit, from +1.5%), worth about +0.3%.
  • Done: Cleaving T4 on the bow (+3% attack speed, from +2%), worth about +0.3%.
  • Prosperous in the empty jewelry slots, or Energized if you play solo.

The empty armor slots only take defensive runes: fill them only if you start dying. Skip Marked upgrades unless you farm bosses, and skip CDR runes.

The DPS increase is the Bow Planner's estimate at your current stats, for the item in its slot compared with what you wear now. Rings and boots assume the same stat rolls as the piece they replace, bows assume the same enhancement (+11) as your current bow, and only the effects listed here are counted. Items in the same slot do not stack, so do not add the bows together. Gains from base ATK shrink as your stat points grow.

The Grip, Signet, Marchboots and Ring of the Eternal all reward never missing and never dying, which makes Eagle Sight one of the most valuable points in the Bow tree.

Skip for this build: Stoneward's Vow Ring (turns off all your debuffs, including Hunter's Mark, Parasitic Arrow's vulnerable and the debuff Eagle Sight needs), Patriarch's Coronet (-30% auto-attack damage), Wraith's Vow Ring (autos cost 2 mana and its bonus needs 75% mana), Tyrant's Heart Ring (only works below 30% HP), Wellspring Ring (a party mana item that hurts you below 50% mana), Iron Grip Ring and Vow of Iron Ring (need a sword or a spear), and Loop of Eternity and Eternal Pendant (loot and XP, no damage). Gloves without a special effect, such as Phoenix Grasp, Stinging Gauntlets, Void-Touched Gauntlets and Reaper's Grasp, don't beat the Grip.
