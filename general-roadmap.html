Spend order · points 58 to 70

    After step 3 every pick is worth about the same (+0.17% per point), so the order of steps 4 to 13 is flexible. Aim adds nothing by itself (hit is capped), it is only a stepping stone to column 3 Malice. Path nodes give the same value as the regular nodes (checked against their tooltips).
