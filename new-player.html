How to use this

You earn one mastery point per Mastery level, and the Bow and General trees have separate point pools, so you follow each list on its own. Take the points in order, top to bottom. Each row says where the node is, what to pick and what it does. A range such as 8–15 means that node costs that many points, so keep saving until you can afford it.

The order comes from the planners on this site: at every step it takes the choice that adds the most damage for the points it costs. The route ends on the build that currently tops the party meter, with all 57 points spent by row 57 of each list.

If a node is greyed out, take the node above it first: every node needs the one it hangs from. Tier IV nodes (Rapid Fire IV, Hunter's Mark IV, Parasitic Arrow IV) unlock at Mastery level 60. If a capstone is still locked when its turn comes, spend the points on the next rows and come back to it.

Bow tree, points 1 to 100

General tree, points 1 to 100

The General tree is a grid: Inner Focus and Path of Force sit at the top, and the columns hang below Path of Force. Columns are numbered 1 to 5 from the left, and nodes are counted from the top of their column. A branch hangs off a column and continues from the node it is attached to. Each node has 1 to 4 ranks, one point per rank, so a node showing "ranks to 2" means two points in a row go into that same node.

General points are worth much less than Bow points. Inner Focus is the only really strong one, so the first 4 points here are the ones that matter most; after that, each point adds a fraction of a percent. Do not agonise over the exact order.

Tips

  • Take Inner Focus (General tree) as your imbue. It costs no mana and adds +4% to all damage at 4/4.
  • Resets have been free so far, so if you pick a wrong node you can move the point. Check Combat Stats after a few changes.
  • For gear, runes, stats and Quest Tree advice, see the Bow Build Guide. To test your own changes, use the Bow Planner and the General Planner.
  • The list stops at 100 points. The Bow and General Endgame pages show the same route with the DPS gain at each step.
